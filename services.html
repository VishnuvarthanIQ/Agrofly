FIELD PREPARATION

Field Requirements & Operational Checklist

Ensure a seamless, safe, and efficient spraying operation by reviewing our quick pre-flight checklist.

Acreage Limits

Minimum single plot size is 1 acre. Smallholder plots under 1 acre are easily bundled into contiguous community cluster bookings.

Vehicle Road Access

A tractor path or motorable field bund within 200m of the plot border enables rapid deployment of the drone and rapid battery recharger.

Clean Staging Water

Farmer provides 8–10 litres of clean water per acre at the boundary. Our onboard dual micro-strainers handle final filtration before flight.

Weather Windows

Flights operate when ambient wind speeds remain under 18 km/h without active rain. Early morning (6:00–10:30 AM) is optimal.
