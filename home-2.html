Aerodynamic Physics

Downward Rotor Vortex vs Conventional Spraying

Why downward multi-rotor downwash achieves 3.8x higher foliar adhesion compared to manual knapsack and tractor lances.

Conventional Method

Manual Knapsack / Lance

⚠️
  • Top-Leaf Flooding & Run-off Excessive water volume (150L/acre) pools on upper foliage and drips into soil, wasting up to 50% of active ingredient.
  • Zero Underside Foliar Contact Droplets fall straight down; pests like whiteflies and stem borers sheltering beneath leaves remain completely unharmed.
  • High Operator Chemical Inhalation Farm workers walk through sprayed foliage for 6 hours carrying 20 kg tanks, creating acute toxic exposure risks.
  • Severe Crop Trampling Walking through dense crops breaks mature stalks, compacts soil, and reduces final yield by 3% to 5%.
Efficiency Rating: Only 35–45% effective chemical retention on target foliage.
AgroFly Engineering

Centrifugal Downwash Vortex

✨
  • Canopy Foliage Inversion 60 km/h rotor propwash parts crop foliage, turning leaves so micron mist coats both top and bottom surfaces where pests breed.
  • Ultra-Low Volume (ULV) Adhesion Uses only 8–10L water per acre with 120–160 µm droplets that cling by surface tension without ground drop-off.
  • Zero Human Exposure Pilots control the drone remotely from field boundaries; nobody enters the treated crop zone during spraying.
  • Zero Crop Disturbance Aerial delivery prevents soil compaction and leaves plants 100% undamaged even in waterlogged, muddy fields.
Efficiency Rating: 92–98% targeted foliage retention with uniform droplet deposition.
Precision SOP

Commercial 3-Stage Spraying Workflow

From initial digital perimeter lock to verified GPS telemetry flight certificates in three automated phases.

01

Digital Field Mapping & Hazard Lock

DGCA-licensed pilots scan field boundaries using RTK hand units or high-resolution drone cameras. Trees, electric poles, water channels, and buffer boundaries are locked into the flight controller.

Deliverable: Centimeter geofence boundary & optimized flight path lines.
02

Autonomous Sortie & 4D Radar Following

The drone launches autonomously, maintaining a strict 2.0-meter altitude above undulating canopies via 4D millimeter-wave radar. Centrifugal atomizers adjust spray volume dynamically to ground velocity.

Deliverable: Uniform 6.5m swath coverage with zero overlap or skips.
03

Instant Telemetry & Field Spray Log

Upon landing, the mission telemetry uploads to the cloud. You receive a verified PDF spray certificate detailing exact GPS flight coordinates, hectares treated, chemical volume consumed, and weather parameters.

Deliverable: Printable farmer invoice & satellite-synced spray verification.
