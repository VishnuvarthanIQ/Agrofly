ROTOR DOWNWASH VORTEX
TARGETED DEFENSE

Lethal Pest Knockdown via Aerodynamic Canopy Inversion

Destructive pests like aphids, thrips, whiteflies, and stem borers naturally hide on the undersides of dense crop foliage where gravity-fed manual sprayers cannot reach.

AGROFLY hexacopters solve this through aerodynamic engineering. Our custom carbon composite rotors produce a powerful turbulent downward air vortex that gently inverts the crop leaves as the drone passes overhead. High-speed centrifugal misting discs discharge electrostatically charged 80–120 micron droplets, ensuring thorough coverage on both sides of every leaf.

98% Pest Mortality on Leaf Undersides
Direct contact kills hidden sucking pests without requiring dangerous chemical over-concentration.
Active Millimeter Obstacle & Contour Radars
Maintains a consistent 2-meter standoff distance above undulating crop canopies automatically.
